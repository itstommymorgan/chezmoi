CUSTOM TEXT OBJECTS                                      *tm-custom-textobjects*

Brackets, quotes, tags, function calls, arguments: (from mini.ai)
`a<char>` / `i<char>` - around/inside the object, e.g. `a(`/`i(`, `a"`/`i"`,
`at`/`it` (tag), `af`/`if` (function call), `aa`/`ia` (argument). Seeks
forward on the line if the cursor isn't directly on/in the target, and
expands outward through nesting on repeated use.

Next/last variants of the above: (also mini.ai)
Prefix the target char with `n` (next) or `l` (last/previous) instead of
using it bare.
`an(` / `in(` - the _next_ () and its contents, or just its contents.
`al}` / `il}` - the _previous_ {} and its contents, or just its contents.

Comments: (from nvim-treesitter-textobjects)
`ac` / `ic` - around/inside a comment. Treesitter-based; `ic` isn't defined
for every language's query file, but fails silently rather than erroring
when it's missing.

Indentation: (from nvim-various-textobjs)
`ai` / `ii` - around/inside a block of same-or-greater-indented lines.

Current line, characterwise: (from nvim-various-textobjs)
`a_` / `i_` - the whole line, or just its content excluding leading/trailing
whitespace either way. This used to be `al`/`il`, but those are now claimed
by mini.ai's next/last modifiers above.
